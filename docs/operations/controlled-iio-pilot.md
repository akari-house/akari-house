# AKARI House controlled IIO pilot

## Pilot objective

Prove that a real AKARI Initial Interest Offering can move from Founder setup to Creator settlement without privacy, permission, operational or payment-recording failures.

## Entry criteria

- CI and Launch Gate Preview are green on the deployed commit.
- `/health` reports the expected release and all checks ready.
- Launch Gate Production passes after environment approval.
- D1 recovery evidence is current.
- Email, Telegram, Google OAuth and Turnstile are verified.
- No critical or high-severity unresolved incident exists.

## Cohort

Start with 10 to 15 invited participants:

- 2 Founders
- 5 to 8 Creators
- 2 Investors
- 1 campaign administrator
- 1 moderator
- 1 Superadmin

Use real identities and communication channels, but keep campaign budgets and settlement exposure deliberately limited.

## Pilot journey

1. Founder creates and submits a project.
2. Administrator reviews and publishes it.
3. Founder creates an IIO campaign with dates, deliverables and budget.
4. Creators apply and accept deliverable terms.
5. Administrators shortlist and select participants.
6. Selected Creators submit work links.
7. Moderator approves, rejects or requests correction.
8. One controlled dispute is opened and resolved.
9. Final payouts are calculated and reviewed.
10. External payment or token transfer is recorded with evidence.
11. Final report and Google export are generated.
12. Account export, closure request and private media boundaries are sampled.

## Daily review

Record:

- failed email, Telegram or Google deliveries
- permission denials and unexpected access
- submission and moderation delays
- dispute reasons
- settlement adjustments
- support requests
- browser and mobile defects
- R2 and D1 operational findings

## Expansion gates

Move from 15 to 25 members only when:

- zero critical or high-severity defects remain
- every settlement reconciles
- no private record or R2 object was exposed
- delivery retries and dead letters are understood
- pilot users can complete core journeys without administrator intervention

Move from 25 to 50, and then 100, using the same criteria. Broad public onboarding remains blocked until the first complete IIO has current production evidence and a signed operational review.
